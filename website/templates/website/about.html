ABOUT BMK ACADEMY

A School Built on Family, Education and Service

Baki and Mairam Kwasari Nursery and Primary School, known as BMK Academy, was established to provide a strong educational foundation for the children of SCL staff.

WHO WE ARE

Baki and Mairam Kwasari Nursery and Primary School

BMK Academy is a nursery and primary school established by Dr. Andrew Kwasari and the Kwasari family in honour of their late parents, Baki Kwasari and Mairam Kwasari.

The school was established to provide education for the children of SCL staff, creating an educational environment close to the community in which the families live and work.

BMK began its journey with Nursery 1 and Nursery 2. As the children have grown, the school has continued to grow with them, with the first Nursery 2 pupils now progressing into Primary 1.

THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

Remembering Baki and Mairam Kwasari

The name of the school carries a family legacy. BMK Academy was named in memory of the late parents of Dr. Andrew Kwasari and the Kwasari family.

THE FATHER

Baki Kwasari

His name forms the first part of BMK, preserving his memory within the identity of the school.

THE MOTHER

Mairam Kwasari

Her name forms the second part of BMK, carrying the memory of the family into a new generation.

BMK & SCL

Growing Within a Wider Community

BMK Academy is located within the SCL Farm environment. SCL is a Nigerian agro-allied and regenerative agriculture organisation whose journey began in 2011.

SCL's work today includes regenerative agriculture, integrated crops, trees and livestock systems, agricultural enterprise, technology, markets and sustainable livelihoods.

Within this wider environment, BMK has its own identity and purpose as a nursery and primary school, providing education for the children of SCL staff.

OUR VALUES

BMK Academy is part of the wider SCL Farm community. While our purpose is education, we seek to nurture children within a culture that values integrity, commitment, creativity and active participation.

From values to education

At BMK Academy, these values are expressed through the way we learn, work together, develop character and prepare children for a life of meaningful service.

OUR MOTTO

Education for Service

Education is more than the acquisition of knowledge. It is also an opportunity to develop character, responsibility, confidence and the ability to make a meaningful contribution to others.

At BMK Academy, our motto, Education for Service, expresses an aspiration to help children develop the knowledge, values and abilities they can carry into the next stages of their lives.
