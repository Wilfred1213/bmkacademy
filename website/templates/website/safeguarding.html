OUR COMMITMENT

Creating a Safe Learning Environment.

BMK Academy is committed to providing a learning environment where children are treated with dignity, respect and care.

As a school located within SCL Farm, BMK Academy operates within a wider organisational environment in which safeguarding and the protection of vulnerable persons are important responsibilities.

We believe that children should be able to learn, participate and grow in an environment where their safety and wellbeing are taken seriously.

SCL SAFEGUARDING FRAMEWORK

A Wider Safeguarding Framework.

SCL maintains a formal safeguarding policy that addresses the prevention of harm, protection of vulnerable persons, reporting mechanisms, investigation and response, and accountability.

The SCL safeguarding framework applies across SCL operations and includes protection against child abuse, harassment, discrimination, neglect and other forms of safeguarding misconduct.

BMK provides this link so that members of the school community can access the relevant SCL safeguarding information.

If You Have a Safeguarding Concern

If you have a concern about the safety or wellbeing of a child or another vulnerable person, it is important that the concern is reported through the appropriate safeguarding mechanism.
