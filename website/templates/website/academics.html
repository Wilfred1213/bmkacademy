ACADEMICS

Learning That Grows With Every Child

At BMK Academy, we provide children with a strong educational foundation while supporting their development as confident, responsible and curious learners.

OUR APPROACH

Building Strong Foundations

The early years of a child's education provide an important foundation for future learning. At BMK Academy, we aim to create an environment where children can develop knowledge, confidence, communication skills and positive values.

As children progress from nursery into primary education, our aim is to grow with them and support them through each stage of their educational journey.

Foundational Learning

Building essential knowledge and skills that children can carry into the next stages of their education.

Curiosity

Encouraging children to ask questions, explore ideas and develop an interest in the world around them.

Confidence

Helping children develop the confidence to communicate, participate and express themselves.

Character

Supporting the development of positive values, responsibility and respectful relationships.

THE BMK JOURNEY

From Early Learning to Primary Education

BMK Academy began with Nursery 1 and Nursery 2. As our first learners have grown, the school has continued to develop with them.

01

Nursery

Providing children with an early learning environment where they can begin developing foundational knowledge, confidence and social skills.

02

Primary

Supporting children as they progress into primary education and continue developing academically and personally.

03

Growing Forward

Continuing to develop alongside our learners as they move through their educational journey.

The Whole Child

Education involves more than academic achievement. Children also need opportunities to develop confidence, relationships, responsibility, creativity and positive values.

Knowledge and understanding
Communication and confidence
Social development
Character and responsibility
BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Preparing Children for More Than the Next Class

Our vision of education extends beyond completing lessons and progressing from one class to another. We want children to develop the confidence, values and abilities they can use throughout life.

This connects naturally with our school motto: Education for Service.

OUR MOTTO

Education for Service

We believe that education should help children develop the knowledge, character and abilities they need to make meaningful contributions to their families, communities and society.
