ABOUT BMK ACADEMY

Building Strong Foundations for a Bright Future

Baki and Mairam Kwasari Nursery and Primary School, known as BMK Academy, was established within SCL Farm to provide quality education for the children of SCL staff.

Commissioned on 19 October 2024, BMK Academy began with Nursery 1 and Nursery 2. As our first learners have grown, the school has continued to develop alongside them, with our Nursery 2 pupils now progressing into Primary 1.

Strong Educational Foundations

Giving children the knowledge, confidence and learning habits they need for the next stages of their educational journey.

Growing With Our Learners

Developing alongside our pupils as they move from the nursery stage into primary education.

Education for Service

Building character, responsibility and a willingness to use knowledge and abilities in meaningful service to others.

OUR ACADEMIC APPROACH

Learning That Grows With Every Child

At BMK Academy, we understand that children develop at different stages and in different ways. Our learning journey is designed to provide a strong foundation while supporting children as they progress through their education.

From the early years of Nursery education to the beginning of Primary school, we aim to create an environment where children can build knowledge, develop confidence and grow as learners.

Learn About Admissions

Foundational Learning

Helping children develop the essential knowledge and skills they need as they progress through their education.

Curiosity & Discovery

Encouraging children to ask questions, explore ideas and develop an interest in the world around them.

Confidence & Expression

Supporting children as they develop confidence in communicating their ideas, participating and expressing themselves.

Whole-Child Development

Recognising that education is about more than academic knowledge and includes the development of positive values and social skills.
